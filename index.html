The Uncanny Valley

Everything our world has lost is still somewhere.

What it is

The Uncanny Valley is a dimension created because of certain things that happened in our world. It's a replica of our world from 20 years ago, but it's different, and changed.

Every person who has ever died in our world, every place that has ever burned down, everything that was destroyed 20 years ago or even longer ago than that: all of it is in this parallel universe. Meet some of them.

The Big Ville

You start in the regular dimension, our plane of society. From there you go to the gateway, almost like the Upside Down in Stranger Things. The gateway to the Uncanny Valley is called the Big Ville.

It's full of entities that will try to kill you.

The ten circles

If you somehow survive the Big Ville, you reach the Uncanny Valley itself, and you have to go through its ten circles.

Each circle is literally a loop. You can never escape it, because you keep ending up in the same exact spot: the spot where you came in. Except every time you arrive back at that spot, it's a little more uncanny.

  1. 10th The spot where you came in.
  2. 9th The same spot. Something is slightly off.
  3. 8th The same spot.
  4. 7th The same spot.
  5. 6th The same spot?
  6. 5th The same spot.
  7. 4th The same spot. You can't remember why you came.
  8. 3rd The same spot.
  9. 2nd The same spot.
  10. 1st Home.

The first world

The whole time you've been looping, you've been forgetting everything your life stands for. By the time you reach the first world, if you survive all the others, there's nothing left to forget.

You become part of the ecosystem of the Uncanny Valley.

And if, as one of those parts of the ecosystem, you ever escape the Uncanny Valley, you don't go home. You go into the Big Ville, as an entity.
